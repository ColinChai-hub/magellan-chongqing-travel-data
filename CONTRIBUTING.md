# Contributing

We welcome corrections, fresh primary-source URLs and genuinely useful Chongqing travel facts.

1. Open an issue explaining the record `id`, the proposed change and a **primary source** (official government, attraction operator, UNESCO, transport operator, etc.).
2. For a pull request, edit the relevant CSV and JSON together; maintain existing IDs.
3. Set `last_source_checked` to the date you personally verified the source; do not update the date automatically without checking.
4. Run `python3 scripts/validate.py` and include the result in your PR.
5. Disclose commercial affiliations and any conflicts of interest. Promotional links, SEO spam, paid-link requests, copied reviews and unlicensed photographs will be rejected.
6. Do not add unverified live prices, opening hours, visa rules, safety guarantees, accessibility certifications, precise travel times, or fabricated coordinates.
7. Do not add personal data or credentials.

Maintainers may reject content that is inaccurate, irrelevant, promotional or insufficiently sourced. Contributions to original content are accepted under the repository's license unless otherwise agreed.
