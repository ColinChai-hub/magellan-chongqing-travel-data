Sources and editorial method

We use first-party public references, including the Chongqing Municipal Government, UNESCO World Heritage Centre and China Railway 12306. Individual records provide source URLs and dates of editorial review where applicable.

Descriptions and practical cautions are independent summaries. Read the data methodology and the source list.

Limitations and reuse

This dataset is not a live source for tickets, prices, opening hours, schedules, visa eligibility or accessible routes. Recheck dynamic information with the relevant official operator before travel.

Original editorial and structured dataset content is published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, subject to exclusions for third-party content and trademarks. See the complete license notes.

How to cite or contribute

Suggested citation: Magellan Chongqing Tours, Magellan Chongqing Open Travel Data, v0.1.0 (2026-11-14), GitHub repository.

To report an inaccurate record, open a GitHub issue with the record ID and supporting official source. Follow the contribution guide. The dataset is maintained by a licensed Chongqing-based inbound tour operator and is not an official government publication.
