# First visit to Chongqing: planning notes

This guide summarizes how to use the datasets, not a substitute for live travel advice.

## Plan by geography

Central-city places such as Liziba, Hongyadong, Ciqikou and Danzishi can be grouped into city sightseeing days. Wulong, Dazu, Nanchuan and Qianjiang require separate regional planning. Avoid treating them as adjacent city-center stops.

## Check before departure

- Confirm opening arrangements and ticket rules with the attraction or official local tourism pages.
- Check rail services with the official [12306 English portal](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Verify transfers, terrain and step-free access for your own needs.
- Expect weather differences between the central city, mountain areas and caves.

## World Heritage themes

- [Dazu Rock Carvings](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/912) — historic cliff carvings in several clusters.
- [South China Karst](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1248/) — includes Wulong Karst and Jinfoshan components in Chongqing.
